# Donate to Google TTS For NVDA

Google TTS For NVDA is free, open-source software under the GPL-2.0 license. Donations are completely optional, and you never need to pay to download, use, or update the add-on.

## Brief Project History

Google TTS For NVDA started in July 2026 (`v0.1.5`) with **Nguyễn Anh Đức** and **Đào Đức Trung**, who built the initial releases and merged the earliest community pull requests. **Phạm Hùng Vương** first contributed through pull requests before becoming a direct contributor in `v0.3` and taking over primary maintenance from `v0.4` through `v1.0` and `1.1`, continuing with both maintainer commits and community pull requests.

As noted in [readme.md](readme.md), **Đào Đức Trung** (active at the initial release) and **Nguyễn Anh Đức** (last commits in July–August 2026) have stepped back from developing the add-on. Once donations reach a reasonable amount, a small share will be set aside as a thank-you to both co-authors for starting the project and supporting its early releases.

## Transfer Reference (Memo)

Please include this note with your transfer so it is easy to identify:

`Thanks for Google TTS For NVDA`

* **Why this wording:** Plain ASCII avoids stripped or garbled non-ASCII characters across domestic and international banking networks (such as SWIFT and US ACH). Framing it as a clear thank-you avoids automated reviews meant for registered non-profits (unlike `"Donation"` or `"Charity"`) as well as ambiguous compliance keywords (such as `"Gift"` or standalone `"Support"`) on cross-border transfers and personal PayPal accounts. For domestic transfers within Vietnam, `Ung ho Google TTS Cho NVDA` works fine as well.
